# Biomolecules

Biomolecules are naturally occurring molecules that are essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms.

## Types of Biomolecules

- Carbohydrates
- Proteins
- Lipids
- Nucleic Acids

## Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are the primary source of energy for living organisms.

## Proteins

Proteins are polymers made up of amino acids and perform structural, enzymatic, and regulatory functions.

## Lipids

Lipids are hydrophobic molecules that store energy and form cell membranes.

## Nucleic Acids

DNA and RNA are nucleic acids responsible for storing and transmitting genetic information.

## Enzymes

Enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up biochemical reactions.

## Relationships

- Proteins are made from amino acids.
- DNA stores genetic information.
- RNA helps in protein synthesis.
- Lipids form cell membranes.
- Carbohydrates provide energy.

## Applications

Biomolecules are used in:

- Medicine
- Biotechnology
- Genetics
- Molecular Biology
- Drug Discovery
