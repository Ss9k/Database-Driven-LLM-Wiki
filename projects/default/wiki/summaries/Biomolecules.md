# Biomolecules

## Summary

Biomolecules are naturally occurring molecules essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms. They include carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids, each with distinct functions such as energy source, structural support, and genetic information storage. Proteins, made from amino acids, perform various functions including enzymatic activities, while lipids store energy and form cell membranes. Biomolecules have numerous applications in fields like medicine, biotechnology, and genetics, contributing to advancements in molecular biology and drug discovery. Overall, biomolecules play a crucial role in the functioning of living organisms.
