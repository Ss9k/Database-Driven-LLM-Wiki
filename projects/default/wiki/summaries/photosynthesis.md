# photosynthesis

## Summary

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert sunlight into chemical energy. This process involves the absorption of sunlight by chlorophyll, a green pigment found in plants. Chlorophyll plays a crucial role in photosynthesis, allowing plants to harness energy from the sun. As a result of photosynthesis, glucose is produced, which is a type of chemical energy. Overall, photosynthesis is essential for the survival of green plants and other living organisms.
