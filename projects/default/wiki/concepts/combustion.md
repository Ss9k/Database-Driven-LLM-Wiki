# combustion

## Definition

Combustion is a process where fuel burns, generating high-pressure gases. It occurs inside a combustion chamber, where fuel is ignited, rapidly increasing pressure. This process is a crucial part of internal combustion engines, where it forces a piston downward, producing rotational motion. Proper combustion improves efficiency and reduces emissions, playing a key role in the engine's operation.
