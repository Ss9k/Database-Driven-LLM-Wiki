# heat engine

## Definition

A heat engine is a device that converts chemical energy into mechanical work. It is utilized in an Internal Combustion Engine, where fuel is burned inside a combustion chamber to produce work. The engine's components, including a cylinder and piston, facilitate this process, enabling the conversion of energy. This process allows the heat engine to generate mechanical work by harnessing the energy released from the fuel.
