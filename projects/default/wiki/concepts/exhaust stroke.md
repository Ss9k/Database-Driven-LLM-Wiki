# exhaust stroke

## Definition

The exhaust stroke is a phase in an engine's operation where burnt gases are expelled from the cylinder. It occurs after the power stroke, when the exhaust valve opens, allowing the burnt gases to leave the cylinder. This process is a key part of the engine's cycle, specifically following the combustion of fuel, and is mentioned in the context of both diesel and petrol engines.
