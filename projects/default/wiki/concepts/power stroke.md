# power stroke

## Definition

The power stroke refers to a phase in an engine's operation. It occurs when combustion rapidly increases pressure and forces the piston downward after the spark plug ignites the mixture near the end of compression, ultimately driving the piston downward.

## Contradictions


### Conflict

**Existing Claim**

The power stroke refers to a phase in an engine's operation. It occurs when combustion rapidly increases pressure and forces the piston downward after the spark plug ignites the mixture near the end of compression, ultimately driving the piston downward.

**New Claim**

High temperature due to compression causes self-ignition without requiring a spark plug

**Reason**

The existing wiki page defines 'the power stroke' by stating it occurs 'after the spark plug ignites the mixture'. This implies a spark plug is required for the power stroke. However, the new claim states that ignition (which leads to a power stroke) can occur 'without requiring a spark plug' (as in diesel engines), which contradicts the universality of the spark plug requirement in the wiki's definition of 'the power stroke'.
