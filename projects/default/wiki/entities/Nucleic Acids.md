# Nucleic Acids

## Summary

Nucleic Acids are DNA and RNA, responsible for storing and transmitting genetic information. They are one of the four main types of biomolecules, along with carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids. RNA helps in protein synthesis, while DNA stores genetic information. Nucleic acids play a crucial role in the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms.
