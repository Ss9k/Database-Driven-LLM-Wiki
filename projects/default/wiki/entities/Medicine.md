# Medicine

## Summary

Biomolecules are naturally occurring molecules essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms. They include carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids, each with distinct functions such as energy provision, structural support, and genetic information storage. Biomolecules like enzymes also act as biological catalysts, speeding up biochemical reactions. They have various applications in fields like medicine and biotechnology.
