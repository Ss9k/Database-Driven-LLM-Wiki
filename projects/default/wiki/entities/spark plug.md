# spark plug

## Summary

A spark plug is a component that ignites the mixture in a combustion chamber. It is used in petrol engines and is responsible for igniting the fuel near the end of compression, causing combustion and rapidly increasing pressure. The spark plug is a crucial part of the engine, as it initiates the power stroke by forcing the piston downward.
