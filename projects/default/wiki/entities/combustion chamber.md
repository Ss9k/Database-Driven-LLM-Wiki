# combustion chamber

## Summary

A combustion chamber is the region where fuel burns and high-pressure gases are generated. It is a critical component of an internal combustion engine, where the chemical energy stored in fuel is converted into mechanical work. Proper combustion in the chamber improves efficiency while reducing emissions, and is achieved through high temperature due to compression, which can cause self-ignition without requiring a spark plug.
