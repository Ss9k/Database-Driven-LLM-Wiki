# cylinder

## Summary

A cylinder is a fundamental component of an Internal Combustion Engine, serving as the combustion chamber. Within the cylinder, fuel is burned to generate mechanical work. During the intake stroke, an air-fuel mixture enters the cylinder. The piston then compresses this mixture inside the cylinder. Combustion rapidly increases pressure within the cylinder, forcing the piston downward during the power stroke. Finally, burnt gases leave the cylinder through the exhaust valve. In diesel engines, only air is compressed within the cylinder before fuel injection.
