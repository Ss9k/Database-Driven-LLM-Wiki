# cylinder

## Summary

A cylinder is a component of an internal combustion engine where the air-fuel mixture is burned to produce mechanical work. It is the space where the piston moves downward, allowing the intake of an air-fuel mixture and the compression of the mixture. The cylinder is also where combustion occurs, rapidly increasing pressure and forcing the piston downward, and where burnt gases leave the engine during the exhaust stroke.
