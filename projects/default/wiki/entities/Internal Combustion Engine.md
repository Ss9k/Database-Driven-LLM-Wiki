# Internal Combustion Engine

## Summary

An Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) is a heat engine that converts the chemical energy stored in fuel into mechanical work by burning the fuel inside a combustion chamber. This engine is characterized by its internal combustion process. The ICE consists of several essential components that facilitate this conversion. These include a cylinder, piston, connecting rod, crankshaft, and valves. Furthermore, the engine incorporates a spark plug, which is specific to petrol engines, and a fuel injector, both crucial for initiating and managing the combustion of fuel within the chamber.
