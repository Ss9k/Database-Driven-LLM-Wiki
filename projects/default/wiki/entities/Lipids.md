# Lipids

## Summary

Lipids are hydrophobic molecules that store energy and form cell membranes. They are one of the four main types of biomolecules, along with carbohydrates, proteins, and nucleic acids. Lipids play a crucial role in the structure and function of living organisms. They are also involved in forming cell membranes, which is essential for cellular integrity.
