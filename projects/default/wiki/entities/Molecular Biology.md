# Molecular Biology

## Summary

Molecular Biology is a field that studies biomolecules, including carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids, which are essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms. Biomolecules have various applications in molecular biology, such as in medicine, biotechnology, and genetics. The study of biomolecules in molecular biology helps in understanding the relationships between different biomolecules, like how proteins are made from amino acids and how DNA stores genetic information. This understanding has led to advancements in drug discovery and other fields.
