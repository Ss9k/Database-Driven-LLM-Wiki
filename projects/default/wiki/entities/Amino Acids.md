# Amino Acids

## Summary

Amino acids are the building blocks of proteins, which are polymers that perform structural, enzymatic, and regulatory functions. Proteins are made up of amino acids, indicating a crucial relationship between the two. Amino acids are essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms. They are a fundamental component of biomolecules, specifically proteins.
