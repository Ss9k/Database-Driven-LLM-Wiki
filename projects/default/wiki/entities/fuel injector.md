# fuel injector

## Summary

A fuel injector is a component of an internal combustion engine that is responsible for delivering fuel into the combustion chamber. It is one of the key components of the engine, working alongside other parts such as the cylinder, piston, and valves. The fuel injector plays a crucial role in the combustion process, which involves burning fuel to produce mechanical work.
