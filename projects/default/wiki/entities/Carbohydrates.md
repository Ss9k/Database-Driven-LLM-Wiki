# Carbohydrates

## Summary

Carbohydrates are the primary source of energy for living organisms. They are one of the main types of biomolecules, which also include proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids. Carbohydrates provide energy, which is essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms. They are a crucial component of biomolecules.
