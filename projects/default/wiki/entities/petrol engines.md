# petrol engines

## Summary

Petrol engines are a type of internal combustion engine that utilizes a spark plug to ignite the fuel. They consist of a cylinder, piston, connecting rod, crankshaft, valves, and fuel injector. A spark plug is required in petrol engines to ignite the fuel, unlike diesel engines which use self-ignition due to high temperature from compression. The spark plug plays a crucial role in the combustion process of petrol engines.
