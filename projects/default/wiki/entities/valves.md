# valves

## Summary

Valves are components of an internal combustion engine that control the flow of air and fuel into the cylinder and exhaust gases out of the cylinder. During the intake stroke, the intake valve opens, allowing an air-fuel mixture into the cylinder, while during compression, both valves remain closed. The valves play a crucial role in regulating the engine's operation, ensuring that the air-fuel mixture enters and exhaust gases exit the cylinder at the correct times.
