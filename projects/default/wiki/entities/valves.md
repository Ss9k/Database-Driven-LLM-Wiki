# valves

## Summary

Valves are components of an internal combustion engine that control the flow of air and fuel into the cylinder and exhaust gases out of the cylinder. During the intake stroke, the intake valve opens, allowing an air-fuel mixture into the cylinder. Both valves remain closed during compression, and they play a crucial role in the engine's operation by regulating the flow of substances in and out of the cylinder.
