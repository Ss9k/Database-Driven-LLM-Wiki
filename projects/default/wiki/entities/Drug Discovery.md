# Drug Discovery

## Summary

Drug discovery utilizes biomolecules, such as proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, and nucleic acids, to develop new medicines. Biomolecules play a crucial role in the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms, making them essential for drug discovery. The understanding of biomolecules and their relationships, including proteins, DNA, RNA, and lipids, is vital for the development of new drugs. Biomolecules are used in drug discovery to create new treatments and therapies.
