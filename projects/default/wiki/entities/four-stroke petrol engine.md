# four-stroke petrol engine

## Summary

A four-stroke petrol engine is an engine that operates through four stages. It operates through four stages: intake, compression, power, and exhaust. During the intake stroke, the piston moves downward while the intake valve opens, allowing an air-fuel mixture into the cylinder, producing rotational motion through the connecting rod and crankshaft.
