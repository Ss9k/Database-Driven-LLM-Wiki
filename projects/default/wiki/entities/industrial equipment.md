# industrial equipment

## Summary

Industrial equipment refers to machinery used in various industries. Internal combustion engines are widely used in industrial equipment, which suggests that it is used for tasks that require significant power. The use of internal combustion engines in industrial equipment is likely due to their high power density and mature manufacturing technology. This allows for efficient operation and quick refueling, making industrial equipment suitable for a range of industrial applications.
