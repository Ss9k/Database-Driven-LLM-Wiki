# industrial equipment

## Summary

Industrial equipment refers to machinery used in various industries. Internal combustion engines are widely used in industrial equipment, which suggests that it is used for tasks that require high power density. The use of internal combustion engines in industrial equipment also implies that it is often used for applications where quick refueling is necessary. This equipment is utilized in various settings, including those where combustion processes are crucial for efficient operation.
