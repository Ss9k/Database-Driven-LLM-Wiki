# Proteins

## Summary

Proteins are polymers made up of amino acids and perform structural, enzymatic, and regulatory functions. They are one of the types of biomolecules, which are naturally occurring molecules essential for living organisms. Proteins are made from amino acids and play a crucial role in various biological processes. They are also involved in enzyme activity, as enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up biochemical reactions.
