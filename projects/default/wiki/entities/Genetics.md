# Genetics

## Summary

Genetics is related to biomolecules as DNA stores genetic information and RNA helps in protein synthesis. Biomolecules are essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms, including genetics. Genetics is also one of the applications of biomolecules, along with medicine, biotechnology, and molecular biology. Biomolecules play a crucial role in genetics, particularly in the storage and transmission of genetic information.
