# generators

## Summary

Generators are devices that utilize internal combustion engines, which provide high power density and mature manufacturing technology, allowing for quick refueling and efficient operation, and are used in various applications, including industrial equipment. Proper combustion in these engines improves efficiency while reducing emissions, making generators a reliable choice for power generation. They are widely used in various industries, including agriculture and shipping, due to their high power density and quick refueling capabilities.
