# air-fuel mixture

## Summary

An air-fuel mixture is a combination of air and fuel that is drawn into the cylinder of an engine. The mixture is created during the intake stroke when the intake valve opens, allowing air and fuel to enter the cylinder. The air-fuel mixture is then compressed by the piston, preparing it for the next stage of the engine's operation. The composition of the air-fuel mixture is crucial for the proper functioning of the engine.
