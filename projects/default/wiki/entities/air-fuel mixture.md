# air-fuel mixture

## Summary

An air-fuel mixture is a combination of air and fuel that is drawn into an engine's cylinder. During the intake stroke, this mixture is allowed into the cylinder, and then it is compressed by the piston. The mixture is composed of air and fuel, which is essential for the engine's operation. The compression of the air-fuel mixture is a critical step in preparing it for the next stage of the engine's operation.
