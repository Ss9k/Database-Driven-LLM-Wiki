# Biotechnology

## Summary

Biomolecules are naturally occurring molecules essential for the structure, function, and regulation of living organisms. They include carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids, each with distinct functions such as energy provision, structural support, and genetic information storage. These biomolecules are crucial for various biological processes and have applications in fields like medicine, biotechnology, and genetics. They are also involved in biochemical reactions, with enzymes acting as catalysts to speed up these processes.
